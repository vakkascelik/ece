Doorway — identity, app icons and store assets

Built on the same shipped tokens as the product: green #2f6f4f, ink #1b1a18, bg #faf9f7. The mark is two shapes — a circle and a bar — because it has to survive a 16px favicon and a monochrome Android themed icon. The name is Doorway — it names the moment the product serves: a child handed from whānau to kaiako at the door, and the ratio limit you do not cross. Swap the wordmark and nothing else in this board changes. I have deliberately not built a te reo Māori name or a koru-derived mark: that is a decision for the centres and mana whenua, not for a design pass.

Caption band takes the state colour of the screen it describes, at the soft end of the pair. Real captures go in the frame — never a redrawn approximation, and never a photo of a child in a store listing.
Do not
Put a gradient behind the mark.
Rotate, outline or add a shadow to the mark.
Recolour the mark to a state colour — breach red on a launcher icon is a lie.
Set the wordmark in a display or script face.
Use a photograph of a child anywhere in marketing without written, current consent from that child's whānau.
Use a koru, kōwhaiwhai or any Māori motif without mandate. The macrons in the product copy are correct usage; borrowed iconography is not.
