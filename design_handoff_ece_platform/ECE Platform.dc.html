a11y — offline & refusal
offline banner: role=status, aria-live=polite, "Offline. 3 sign-ins waiting to send."
chip: text node, not a title attribute
dialog: role=dialog aria-modal, labelled by the h4, focus trapped, initial focus "Try sending now" (48px)

rationale
No "sign out anyway". A destructive escape hatch on a screen an educator taps forty times a day is how attendance gets lost. Blue, not amber: a queue is normal.

a11y — mobile
sign in/out: role=button, 88×56, label "Sign Aroha Ngata out"
consent switch: role=switch, 56×32 visual inside a 56px row target, label "Photos in the pānui feed"

withdrawn photo: nothing rendered, nothing announced — no empty figure, no alt text
rationale
The whānau child screen has no custody heading at all. An empty "Custody" section on a parent's phone tells that parent an order exists — or that one does not. Neither is ours to say.

5 · Tablet at three metres

Sign-in tablet, 11″ landscape, wall-mounted by the door. The ratio block is sized so its status line subtends the same angle at 3 m as 15px body text does at 40 cm — roughly 112px of cap height on a 10.5″-wide panel. The proof is the second panel: the same render at 13% scale is the retinal size of the first at three metres.
